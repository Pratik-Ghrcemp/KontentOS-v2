My strongest recommendation
Don't turn the current KontentOS into a giant collection of AI features.
Instead, make it behave like a virtual social-media/content manager.
The creator should be able to open KontentOS every morning and ask:
"What should I create today?"
KontentOS answers:
"Based on your audience, recent performance and current trends, here are the 3 things you should create."
Creator picks one.
KontentOS:
researches → writes → scripts → prepares shot list → helps record → edits → repurposes → creates captions → schedules → publishes → measures → learns.
Then tomorrow:
"Here's what you should make next."
That is a product people can potentially pay for every month.
And the strongest long-term positioning is:
KontentOS
Your AI Creator Manager
Think. Create. Publish. Grow. Earn.
The existing recording-to-marketing-kit engine becomes one powerful subsystem inside that larger Creator OS, rather than the product itself.
